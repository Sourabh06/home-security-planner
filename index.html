Camera planner

Plan a home CCTV setup in three steps: draw your house, check camera coverage and blind spots, then estimate the cost.

Step 1: Draw your house

Explore the example floor plan first, then tap Try out your floor plan (or Your plan) to start drawing and move to Camera & blind spots.

Step 2: Camera & blind spots

Draw your walls and room labels, place indoor and outdoor cameras, use Suggest to find corner positions, and adjust Field of view and Range to spot blind spots.

Step 3: Cost estimation

Set a budget, choose a recording system, and add cameras manually or auto-populate from your 2D floor plan.

    What data is captured?

    This site has no accounts, tracking or analytics, and nothing you enter is sent to a server. Your budget, camera choices, prices and floor plan are saved only in your own browser and can be cleared by clearing site data. The 3D view uses your phone’s camera and motion sensors to show AR on your device; the camera view is not uploaded. The 3D page loads a code library from the jsDelivr CDN, and your hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as your IP address.
